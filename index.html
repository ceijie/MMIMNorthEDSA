Who We Are

A local church under Messiah Mission International Ministries

MMIM North EDSA is a local church under Messiah Mission International Ministries (MMIM). As part of the MMIM family, we are committed to proclaiming the Gospel of Jesus Christ, making disciples, planting churches, and equipping believers to faithfully serve God in their homes, communities, and the nations.


Our desire is to see lives transformed through the saving grace of Jesus Christ and to build a community where people grow together in faith, love, and obedience to God's Word.

Our Story

May 16, 2002

Messiah Mission International Ministries was founded to fulfill the Great Commission of Jesus Christ.

Expansion Years

Weekly worship services and discipleship gatherings strengthened believers and expanded ministry outreach.

July 15, 2004

MMIM officially established its General Leadership under Rev. Reynaldo T. Bechayda as President.

September 13, 2004

MMIM was officially registered with the SEC as a religious organization in the Philippines.

Today

MMIM continues planting churches, equipping believers, and advancing God's Kingdom through evangelism, discipleship, missions, and compassionate service.

Why We Exist

We exist to REACH people for Jesus by Equipping every believer, developing their Abilities in serving the Lord, and Caring for one another in order to Honor God's name.

Our Mission

To REACH the whole Philippines, 10/40 window countries, and the world through intentional evangelism and missions, resulting in the planting of 500 viable congregations by the year 2030.

Our Statement of Faith

1. Bible

The Holy Bible is the inspired and infallible Word of God.

2. God

One Living and True God revealed as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

3. Jesus Christ

Jesus is the Messiah, Savior of mankind, who died, rose again, ascended, and reigns with the Father.

4. The Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit sanctifies, empowers believers, and convicts the world of sin.

5. Man

Created in God's image yet fallen because of sin.

6. Salvation

Salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ.

7. Church

The church exists to reach people, equip believers, and honor God's name.

8. Last Days

Jesus Christ will return and establish His kingdom.

9. Eternal State

All people will be resurrected for eternal life or eternal judgment.
